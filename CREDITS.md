## Sprites

`apps/Sokoban/Art/sprites` (packed into `apps/Sokoban/Assets/sprites.png`) contains sprites from **"50 Sokoban assets"** by [1001.com](https://www.1001.com),
licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
The sprites are unmodified; modified versions must be shared under the same license.

## Font

`shell/Assets/fonts/LilitaOne-Regular.ttf` is **Lilita One** by Juan Montoreano, licensed under the
[SIL Open Font License 1.1](shell/Assets/fonts/OFL.txt).

## Sounds

`shell/Assets/sounds/` holds `navigate.wav` and `completed.wav` from [coenvk/Sokoban](https://github.com/coenvk/Sokoban);
`completed.wav` was converted from 24-bit to 16-bit PCM, which MonoGame requires. Their original source is not recorded
there, so check their license before distributing the game.

## Levels

`levels/original.xsb` holds the 100 levels from [coenvk/Sokoban](https://github.com/coenvk/Sokoban), converted to XSB.
