Games on Jentl

Six small games in your browser, made with the Jentl engine. They also run on the desktop, and in Dutch (Options > Language).

Sokoban

Push every crate onto a goal. Two packs of levels, generated levels, and hints from a solver.

Solitaire

Nine games: Klondike, Easthaven, Forty Thieves, FreeCell, Golf, Pyramid, Spider, TriPeaks and Yukon.

Card Table

Uno and Texas Hold'em against bots, passing the device round, or Uno online.

Mossbrook

A small turn-based adventure: a village, a cellar and a lost amulet.

Code Lock

Mastermind on a combination lock: crack the code in ten tries.

Bricks

A brick breaker with power-ups, on Jentl's entity component system and physics.
Source: the games and the Jentl engine.
