What should a car card look like?

Three invented cars in two styles. The colour of the frame is the rarity class (F grey up to S gold); RQ is the car's strength, which counts towards your hand's limit. Click the style you like.

A

Full card (like Top Drives)

Every stat on the card; used when looking at a car, in the garage and before a race.

B

Compact card, full details on tap

Smaller cards so more fit on screen (a hand of five on a phone, a big garage grid); tap one for the full card.
